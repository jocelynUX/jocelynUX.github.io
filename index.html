Hi, I'm Jocelyn

I make complex systems easier to understand, use, and improve.

I connect research, data, documentation, and implementation to solve practical problems across products and operations.

My background spans software development, UX research, accessibility, localization, documentation, and data analysis. I’m especially drawn to the places where work gets tangled: when a team needs to understand what people are experiencing, make sense of incomplete information, choose or configure the right tools, or create a process that others can actually follow.


I approach those problems by asking careful questions, looking closely at the evidence, and staying connected to the practical details. Depending on the need, that might mean conducting research, analyzing usage patterns, testing an interface, mapping a workflow, documenting a technical process, or helping a new system fit more clearly into the way people work.

How I Work

I use research, data, technical investigation, and documentation to understand complicated problems and make them more manageable. My role changes with the problem, but my work often falls into four connected areas.

Understanding People and Problems

I begin by learning how people experience a product, process, or environment. I look for needs, constraints, assumptions, and points of confusion that may not be visible from inside the system.

Methods and activities

  • Semi-structured interviews
  • Usability testing
  • Literature and background research
  • Scenario development
  • Accessibility and inclusive-design review
  • Research synthesis

In practice

For Urban Gems, our team used interviews to understand why people who wanted to explore their neighborhoods still struggled to try unfamiliar places.

See the Urban Gems project

Analyzing Information and Patterns

I organize qualitative and quantitative information so that patterns, gaps, and useful questions become easier to see. I also consider the limitations and practical constraints surrounding a decision.

Methods, tools, and activities

  • Data cleaning and preparation
  • Tableau and Tableau Prep
  • Trend and cluster analysis
  • Data visualization
  • Qualitative synthesis
  • Comparison and evaluation frameworks

In practice

At Pluto VR, I analyzed regional product-usage data in Tableau to identify countries with similar growth patterns and help clarify possible priorities for localization.

See the regional analysis project

Improving Products and Processes

I use what I learn to identify practical changes to an interface, workflow, or service. I consider both the people using a system and the people responsible for maintaining it.

Methods, tools, and activities

  • Journey and workflow mapping
  • Prototyping in Figma
  • Iterative usability testing
  • Accessibility review
  • Information-flow analysis
  • Process clarification

In practice

During Urban Gems field testing, feedback led our team to clarify location categories, reorganize detail screens, and give people more control over their recommendations.

See how the prototype evolved

Supporting Implementation and Adoption

A recommendation is only useful when people can apply it. I help connect an idea to the technical workflow, documentation, and shared understanding needed to put it into practice.

Methods, tools, and activities

  • Tool and vendor evaluation
  • Technical testing
  • Workflow design
  • Localization and internationalization
  • Process documentation
  • Training and onboarding materials

In practice

For Pluto VR’s localization work, I researched compatible tools, tested the translation workflow, and documented how developers and translators would complete their parts of the process.

See the localization workflow

Building Pluto VR’s Localization Workflow

Pluto VR needed a localization process that worked with its React product and gave engineers and translators clearer handoffs. I researched and evaluated tools, tested and implemented the workflow, collaborated with engineers and a linguist, and created documentation to help the team use it.

Using Product Data to Guide Pluto VR’s Regional Priorities

Pluto VR needed to understand where PlutoSphere usage was growing and whether those patterns could guide infrastructure and localization decisions. I cleaned and analyzed six months of geographic usage data in Tableau, adapted the work when infrastructure constraints blocked the original goal, and presented recommendations to leadership.

  • Tableau & Tableau Prep
  • Data preparation
  • Geographic analysis
  • Constraint-aware problem solving
  • Leadership communication

Urban Gems: Designing Discovery Without Decision Fatigue

People wanted to discover unfamiliar places near home, but decision fatigue and generic recommendations made exploration feel like work. I contributed to interviews, personas, journey mapping, Figma prototyping, and in-context testing, then helped iterate the concept to balance spontaneity with clearer choices and user control.

  • Personas & journey mapping
  • Figma prototyping
  • In-context testing
